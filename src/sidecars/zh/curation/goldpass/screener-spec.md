# Screener specification — native-checker gold pass (issue #20, AC7)

Run as a short, separately-paid Prolific screener study; only passers are
invited to the main task. Target: **native Mandarin speakers who are
Traditional-script literate and pinyin-competent** (the item claims are in
Hanyu Pinyin).

## Prolific prescreen filters (set in the study settings)

- First language: Chinese (Mandarin).
- Country of birth / current residence: include Taiwan and Hong Kong;
  include others only in combination with the in-task screen below
  (Traditional literacy is the binding requirement, not geography).
- Fluent languages: English (the instructions are English).

## In-task screen (5 items, ~2 minutes; pass = 5/5)

1. **Traditional-script reading:** show 「這個學生喜歡看書。」 and four
   English paraphrases; exactly one is correct.
2. **Traditional-vs-Simplified discrimination:** show the pair 學/学 and ask
   which form belongs in a Taiwan newspaper.
3. **Pinyin tone competence:** 「媽 is pronounced…」 with options mā / má /
   mǎ / mà.
4. **Context reading (the skill the main task needs):** 「吃不了 — the
   reading of 了 here is…」 options le / liǎo / la / lou. (Answer: liǎo.)
5. **Tone sandhi awareness:** 「不去 is pronounced…」 options bù qù / bú qù /
   bǔ qù. (Answer: bú qù.)

## Disqualifiers

- Any wrong answer on items 1–3 (hard literacy/pinyin floor).
- Wrong on BOTH 4 and 5 (sandhi/context competence floor; one miss on 4/5
  is tolerated only with a perfect 1–3).
- Completion time under 30 seconds (straight-lining).

## Notes

- Pay the screener at the platform's fair-rate floor regardless of outcome.
- Do not reveal which answers were wrong (the same items screen future
  cohorts).
- Record the screener pass-rate in the AC7 provenance record (it is part of
  checker provenance: who checked, how they were qualified).
